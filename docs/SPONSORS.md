# Support Soloblog

Soloblog is free and open source - anyone can use it, and see and change
how it's made. One person makes it and looks after it. Your support is
what keeps it going: the time for new versions, security fixes,
answering questions and keeping this guide up to date.

## Support it every month

A small amount every month, of any size, is the most useful kind of
support: it means the time for Soloblog can be planned ahead.

- **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zrplus_studio)** - choose an amount and
  **Make this monthly**, by card or PayPal.

## A one-off thank you

If Soloblog saved you time or money, a one-off amount is welcome too.

- **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zrplus_studio)** - any amount, once, by
  card or PayPal. No account needed.

## Support it as a company

If your company hosts blogs with Soloblog, builds on it, or would like
its name in front of people setting up their own site, your support can
come with your logo and a link in the project's README and on this page.
Get in touch through [GitHub](https://github.com/soloblog).

## Other ways to help

These cost nothing, and help just as much:

- **Tell people about Soloblog** - in a post on your own site, or with a
  link to the project.
- **Report problems and suggest improvements** - **Report a problem** and
  **Ideas and questions** are at the bottom of every admin page.
- **Improve a translation** - the words visitors see on your site are in
  23 languages, and a native speaker's eye helps. Suggest a better word
  under **Ideas and questions**.
- **Star the project on GitHub**, so others find it.
- **Use the referral links** in the
  [hosting guide](DEPLOYMENT.md#about-the-links-in-this-guide) when you
  sign up for hosting or a domain. Some hosts give the project a credit or
  a small payment, at no extra cost to you.
