## [1.0.0-beta] - 2026-11-17

The first public release: a blog and personal website for one person,
that you run yourself - one Docker image and one folder of data. What's
in it, in short (the [user guide](docs/guide/README.md) has the rest):

- **Write in markdown, comfortably** - a preview that matches the
  published post, a slash menu, Focus, drafts, scheduling, private
  preview links and the last 50 versions of every post - a Go to
  menu that goes to any part of a page, and your posts' titles suggested
  as you type a link to one.
- **Custom colours** - any theme made in daisyUI's theme generator,
  pasted in beside the 35 built-in palettes, its text kept readable.
- **Templates and sections** - 31 templates to start a page from, or add
  to one you've written - a recipe, a resume, a landing page, a project, a
  wiki note, slides and more - and your own, saved from any post. A
  page is built from 41 kinds of section: a recipe's facts, ingredients
  and method, an interview as a chat, a series' contents, previous and
  next pages, a portfolio with your work pictures first (in a grid, in
  rows edge to edge, or a screen each), a scroll story - pictures that
  change as the words scroll, the words fading in and out - features, numbers,
  testimonials, pricing, products, a team, questions, columns, a comparison
  table, your latest posts, a sign-up box, a contact form and more - each
  as wide as you like, on a card or as a full-screen cover, with its
  heading on the left or centred, its own room around it and its own way
  of coming into view (set for the site, a page or one section), each
  added with all its options written out and faded until you change one -
  and buttons, pull quotes, countdowns and folds inside the text.
- **Make it yours** - 35 colour palettes with dark mode, fonts, header
  and footer styles, a menu and a footer in the order you choose
  (Home, Blog and pages like the wiki, the archive or the contact form
  too, each renamed if you like), side panels, the space between
  sections, centred section headings and twelve starting styles,
  previewed before you publish.
- **Settings that show what's different** - a page's title (left,
  centred or hidden) and width, and in one fold the settings few pages
  use (slides, the wiki, a reminder to update, a link) - with a coloured
  line and a Reset button on whatever a page sets differently from the
  site, counted in the panel's heading; and on every settings page, the
  same on whatever differs from your starting style.
- **Media** - galleries, a library with alt text and credits, WebP and
  smaller copies made for you, stock photos, audio and video - and a dark
  mode version, and a phone version (with a dark one of its own), for any
  picture.
- **Readers and members** - comments with replies and moderation, a
  newsletter (each post, or weekly or monthly, designed for email apps),
  a contact form, and free or paid members through Stripe.
- **Found and followed** - RSS, the fediverse, webmentions, Bluesky and
  Mastodon sharing, search-engine ready, in 23 languages.
- **Bring your blog** - import from WordPress, Ghost, Medium, Substack,
  or a folder of markdown.
- **Apps and AI assistants** - an API, Micropub, an MCP server and a
  Ghost-compatible layer, with drafts-only keys.
- **Safe to run** - backups (to a cloud bucket too), passkeys and
  two-factor login, "Keep me signed in" to untick on a shared computer, Site health with a problems log and a detailed log to
  download, updates that never need anything from you, and nothing sent
  anywhere unless you turn it on.
- **Help built in** - a guide by task, from setting up to running your
  site, with a reference for every setting, searchable from any admin
  page.
- **Set up anywhere** - one-click hosts, your own server or a computer at
  home, or one prompt for an AI agent to do it with you.
