# Code of conduct

Soloblog follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in its issues, Discussions, pull requests and anywhere else it's
discussed.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and remember that
many people here are new to running their own site. No harassment,
insults or personal attacks, and nothing that makes someone feel
unwelcome.

**Reporting:** if someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainer
privately through the [contact form](https://soloblog.zrplus.net/contact)
on the project website. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer
may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow this
code.
